KrushiSevak Marketplace + Real Location Update

Implemented:
1. Farmer can save real GPS coordinates from browser using "Use My Current Location".
2. GPS coordinates are stored in MongoDB User.location and attached to new crop listings.
3. Crop listings support crop photos, price per unit, quantity and unit (kg/quintal/ton/bag).
4. Customer Marketplace has "Use My Location" and radius filters (10/25/50/100/250 km).
5. Nearby available farmer listings are calculated with Haversine distance and sorted nearest-first.
6. Marketplace shows farmer, farm location, available quantity, price/unit, photo and distance.
7. Customer can open Google Maps directions to the farmer's saved GPS location and call the farmer.
8. Only Available crop listings are shown in the marketplace.
9. Existing farmer marketplace purchase/cart flow remains available.
10. Existing MongoDB connection/.env was not changed.
